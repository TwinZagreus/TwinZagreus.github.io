## The useful edge of ambiguity

Clarity is not the same as explaining everything. In a dense system, a little ambiguity can create the tension that keeps the eye moving. The trick is to make the destination clear even when the route is atmospheric.

Consider a simple error function. The equation is familiar, but the way it is framed still changes how we understand it:

$$
\text{MSE} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n (y_i - y_{\text{pred}_i})^2
$$

The formula is a signal. The space around it is what lets the signal be heard.
