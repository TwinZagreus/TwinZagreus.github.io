## The interface before the interface

Most screens begin with a request for attention. This one begins by returning it. The first seconds of a digital experience are not empty time; they are a threshold, a place where the visitor decides whether to stay.

That is why I keep coming back to the pause. A soft entrance can make a complex thing feel legible. A single point of light can be enough to say: there is something here, and it is still becoming.

> Make the first gesture generous. The rest of the interface will feel more considered for it.

## A little room to breathe

The work is less about decoration than about pacing. Motion should give the eye a place to land, then get out of the way. The best transitions are brief acts of orientation.

- Start with one strong visual idea.
- Use contrast to establish a hierarchy before adding detail.
- Let content arrive in a sequence, not all at once.
